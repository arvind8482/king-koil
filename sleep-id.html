The SleepID Spectrum

Your number defines your required support level, from plush pressure relief to rigid spinal support.

ID 1 : Super Soft

Maximum plushness. Deep sink for pressure point relief, ideal for lightweight side sleepers.

ID 2 : Soft

Gentle contouring. A cloud-like feel with underlying support.

ID 3 : Medium Soft

Balanced plushness and push-back. The sweet spot for many sleepers.

ID 4 : Medium Firm

Solid spinal support with essential cushioning layer.

ID 5 : Firm

Rigid support with minimal give. Recommended for back sleepers.

ID 6 : Extra Firm

Maximum rigidity. An unyielding surface designed for orthopedic needs.
